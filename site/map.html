Community map

Every home in Glen Ridge belongs to one of eight sections, and each section has its own rules. Choose your section on the map, or from the list below it, to open the rules for your home.

Map boundaries are approximate. If your home sits on a line, your deed or closing documents list your section.

Find your section

The map shows the eight sections as numbered, colored areas over the neighborhood. Drag it to move, and use the plus and minus buttons to zoom. Everything on it is also in the list of sections below, so you never need the map to reach your rules.
